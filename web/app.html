BOQ Material Takeoff (preliminary)

Paste the BOQ as CSV (header: item_no, description, unit, quantity) or choose a .csv file. Each line is matched to a recipe and the materials needed are calculated with wastage. Lines with no recipe are listed, never guessed.

Layers should be named for the service (for example P-PIPE, M-DUCT, E-CONDUIT, F-PIPE) and sizes written beside the runs (DN50, 400x300). DWG and PDF drawings are not measured yet.
