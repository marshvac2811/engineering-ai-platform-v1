# Phase 14 — Plumbing + Construction/Structural Scope

Adds governed NBC 2016 scope references for:
- Part 9 plumbing: water supply, drainage/sanitation, solid waste, gas
- Part 6 structural: loads and soils/foundations
- Part 7 construction management, practices and safety

These are deliberately scope references, not fabricated PASS/FAIL rules. A
numerical compliance result requires a structured requirement and sufficient
project inputs.
